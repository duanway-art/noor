Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Noor Quran mobile application (the “App”) handles information when you use it. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

The App is provided as a spiritual reading and worship companion. Contact for privacy questions should be directed to the developer or publisher shown in the App Store listing.

2. Information the App processes

Quran text and search. Arabic Quranic text and translations in your selected language are stored in the App bundle (local data). Search runs entirely on your device; search queries are not sent to our servers.

Reading progress. Your last-read surah and verse may be saved locally on your device using system storage (e.g. UserDefaults) so you can resume reading. This data stays on your device unless you back up your device to iCloud or another backup service under Apple’s policies.

Ayah of the Day. A verse may be selected for display based on the calendar date on your device. No personal account is required.

3. Location and sensors

Location. If you grant permission, the App uses your approximate or precise device location to:

  • calculate Islamic prayer times for your geographic position;
  • determine the Qibla (direction to the Kaaba in Makkah) and show a compass-style indicator.

Location is processed on your device for these features. We do not operate backend servers that receive your GPS coordinates from the App for advertising or profiling in this version of the App.

Compass / heading. The Qibla screen may use the device magnetometer and heading APIs. This is used only to orient the compass relative to geographic or magnetic north.

4. Network access

The App may load remote images (for example decorative artwork in certain screens) over HTTPS. The Quranic text used for reading is loaded from the App bundle, not downloaded as primary content during normal reading.

If you use features that rely on the network, standard network diagnostics may apply at the operating system level.

5. Analytics and advertising

This version of the App does not include third-party advertising SDKs or behavioral analytics as part of the codebase described in this policy. If that changes in a future release, this policy will be updated and the App Store listing will be updated accordingly.

6. Children

The App is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what the device and Apple provide under standard parental controls and App Store rules.

7. Your choices

  • You can revoke location access at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services on your device.
  • You can delete the App to remove locally stored reading progress and other on-device data (subject to your device backup settings).

8. International users

Information processed by the App stays primarily on your device. If you use iCloud backup, data may be stored according to Apple’s terms and your account region.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For privacy-related requests, please use the contact method provided on the App Store product page.
